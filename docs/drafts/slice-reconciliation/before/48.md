## QA execution plan

Each indented check belongs to its bold parent.
`↳` shows that relationship; `→` connects the condition to its check.
Adjacent checks name the same wallet, action or state where they are related.

Use a separate result record for each scenario.
Record its starting state, action, expected result and actual result on separate lines.
Record PASS, FAIL or BLOCKED, the source revision and a sanitized evidence link.
An unchecked or blocked item cannot be counted as passed.

## What to build

Let a participant contribute genuine tMIX from verified local state without an online Backup Account
or Sponsor Service, and recover the action after restarting.

Complete this narrow journey through the contract/runtime, application, interface and relevant
verification boundaries; reuse existing seams rather than creating separate layer tickets.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Require a downloaded Recovery Kit and a successful local export/restore before the first
  contribution; include actual witness material and explain that support cannot recover missing
  secrets.
- [ ] Enforce the 1 tMIX minimum, six-decimal exactness and 1,000 total outstanding Principal per
  stable deployment credential from the first contribution. Financial truth and custody are
  contract-enforced; Principal and Prize reserves stay segregated.
- [ ] Atomically save versioned encrypted state and action intent before submission. Failed writes
  prevent submission; restart and final-persistence failures preserve the pending journal and
  reconcile the original action.
- [ ] Use confirmed boundaries for interval weight and automatic offline carry-forward. Disclose
  return-to-check/claim requirements and no Preprod email/push prize alerts before consent.
- [ ] Show the two disclosure cards from #71 (withdrawing links back to your deposit; the Operator
  picks the winner) before the first contribution. Contribute stays disabled until both separate
  acknowledgements are given. Key them by deployment and notice version, ask again when either
  changes, and store them only in local encrypted private state.
- [ ] Keep keys in session memory, isolate network/deployment/account identity and exclude competing
  writers from the start. Verify success, invalid amount/cap, rejected authorization, storage
  failure and crash points end to end.

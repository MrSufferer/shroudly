## What to build

Let a participant withdraw part or all of genuine Principal while retaining every valid historical
draw entitlement.

Complete this narrow journey through the contract/runtime, application, interface and relevant
verification boundaries; reuse existing seams rather than creating separate layer tickets.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Accept any positive representable six-decimal amount up to Principal, including full
  withdrawal and a remainder below 1 tMIX; reject zero, excess and invalid precision without
  transfer.
- [ ] Restore contribution capacity, preserve elapsed interval weight and zero future weight after
  full withdrawal. Do not spend Prize liabilities or impose a backlog-only exit condition.
- [ ] Carry durable prepare/reconcile and privacy guarantees through the withdrawal; preserve
  unrelated valid claims and safe exits when another action is unknown. The withdraw step states
  that it publicly links back to the deposit (public retirement, ADR 0031); the disclosure
  acknowledgements never block a withdrawal.
- [ ] Verify balances/custody, partial/full withdrawals, recovery, stale witnesses and the
  corresponding browser states against contract truth.

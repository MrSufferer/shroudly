## What to build

Provision the Render Sponsor Service and read-only Chain Data Proxy environments, the Blockfrost
accounts, and the bounded DUST credential, independently of
completed financial implementation.

Human setup/review gate.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Restrict the hot credential to capped sponsorship; it has no Principal, Prize, governance,
  emergency or randomness powers.
- [ ] Record provider variable names, least-privilege locations, health validation, safe secret
  handling, rotation/recovery and free-tier sleep/wake/quota behavior without exposing values.
- [ ] Validate hosting access and service connectivity with sanitized evidence. Runtime
  authorization, accounting and persistence are part of the sponsored-action slice.

## Required human inputs

- [ ] Authorize the Render project and capped DUST sponsorship credential.
  - Record who can deploy, rotate credentials and recover the service.
  - Keep sponsorship, governance, emergency and randomness roles separate.
- [ ] Supply server-only sponsor configuration through the provider secret store.
  - `MIDNIGHT_SPONSOR_UPSTREAM_URL`
  - `MIDNIGHT_SPONSOR_UPSTREAM_TOKEN`
  - `MIDNIGHT_SPONSOR_DEPLOYMENT_ID`
  - `MIDNIGHT_SPONSOR_CONTRACT_IDS`
  - `MIDNIGHT_SPONSOR_CIRCUIT_IDS`
  - Reuse the authorized Supabase server credentials from the backup setup gate.
- [ ] Authorize the isolated automation environments and monitoring account.
  - Provide access to protected GitHub Actions environments and the Render signer environment.
  - Keep the offline recovery randomness key and authority keys in separate approved stores.
  - Provide Better Stack monitor/status-page access and `BETTER_STACK_HEARTBEAT_URL`.
  - Record secret reference names and responder ownership without exposing their values.
- [ ] Validate each service connection without printing credentials.
  - Record permissions, DUST spending limits, rotation and account recovery steps.
  - Record free-tier sleep, quota, expiry and billing behavior.

- [ ] Authorize two Blockfrost Midnight Preprod accounts.
  - Public: Chain Data Proxy only. Operator: Sponsor Service, Automation Service and Operator CLI.
  - Record plan tier, 50,000 req/day, 10 req/s per IP with a 500 burst, and the 80% Better Stack
    quota alert. Record that proxy and Sponsor may share a Render egress IP (unverified).
  - Token values live only in provider secret stores; never in the web bundle or evidence.
- [ ] Register every operator wallet (Sponsor, deployer, randomness, evidence) for DUST.
  - Fund tNIGHT, call `registerNightUtxosForDustGeneration`, and wait until DUST is visible.
  - Record the registration-to-DUST delay as `faucetTiming`.
- [ ] Record in the runbook (story 33): all three Preprod randomness keys are held by the same
  maintainer, who also computes interim selection as Operator (ADR 0031). Two-of-three reveals
  protect against one lost key, not against that maintainer. Do not describe provider isolation,
  randomness or selection as independent custody.

This gate provisions access only.
It does not authorize deployment of the product or certify transaction correctness.
